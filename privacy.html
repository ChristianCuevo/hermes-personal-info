Privacy policy

Hermes Personal

Effective date: October 14, 2026.

Purpose and operator

Hermes Personal is a privately operated personal assistant used by its owner. This policy describes the Google account integration for that installation.

Google data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant can access Gmail message and draft contents, headers, recipients and related metadata to find and summarize mail, compose replies, save or edit supported drafts, and send emails. Existing supported draft attachments may be read and retained when preparing an edit or send; their names, types, sizes and integrity fingerprints appear in the review. It can read Google Calendar event details, including dates, locations, descriptions and attendees, and create, change or cancel supported primary-calendar events after approval.

The base connection requests the Gmail read-only (gmail.readonly), Gmail send (gmail.send), and Calendar events (calendar.events) OAuth permissions. Email sending and calendar changes are executed only after the owner approves the complete proposed action.

Draft and inbox management require Gmail modify (gmail.modify) and basic settings (gmail.settings.basic). These permissions allow broader mailbox and settings changes, but the integration exposes only its documented draft, sending, archive and archive-filter operations. It can read supported filters and message metadata to prepare accurate previews. OAuth credentials enable access without giving the assistant the owner's Google password.

How data is used and shared

Google data is used to fulfill the owner's email, scheduling and personal administration requests. Relevant content may be sent to OpenAI for model inference. Responses, summaries and notifications may be delivered through Telegram. The assistant runtime and working data are hosted on DigitalOcean. These service providers process information under their respective policies.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Complete email, draft, calendar and inbox action previews are delivered through a private Telegram group containing the owner, the assistant and the approval bot. Previews may include exact message content, existing draft attachment information, event details, recipients and attendees. The owner must explicitly approve each action; completion updates appear on its approval message. Sending shares the approved email and any retained attachments with its recipients. Calendar changes can send invitations, updates or cancellations to affected guests. An approved filter then archives future matching incoming messages automatically in Gmail until removed. Creating a filter does not archive existing messages, and removing one does not restore previously archived messages to the inbox.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored encrypted on the private server with restricted access. Retrieved content, generated summaries, action previews and execution records may be retained in conversation history, memory, working files, approval records or logs until the owner removes them. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion period for those records. Google, OpenAI, Telegram and the hosting provider maintain their own retention practices.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future API access through that authorization but does not remove filters already created in Gmail, undo earlier mailbox changes, or delete information already stored by the assistant. To stop an existing automatic rule, remove the filter in Gmail or approve its removal through the integration while access remains connected. The owner can remove saved credentials, conversation history, memories, working files and approval records on the private server, and manage retained messages through the relevant service.

This website and contact

This informational website has no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts or connection to the assistant's Google credentials. Its hosting provider may process standard technical request logs. For questions or deletion requests, contact the operator using the support email shown on the application's Google authorization screen.

This policy will be updated if the installation's data practices change.
