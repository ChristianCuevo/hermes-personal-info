Privacy policy

Hermes Personal

Effective date: October 14, 2026.

Purpose and operator

Hermes Personal is a privately operated personal assistant used by its owner. This policy describes the Google account integration for that installation.

Google data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the assistant can access Gmail message contents, headers, recipients and related metadata to find and summarize mail or prepare replies. It can send email on the owner's behalf. It can read Google Calendar event details and create, update or delete events, including associated dates, locations, descriptions and attendees.

The connection requests the Gmail read-only, Gmail send, and Calendar events OAuth permissions. OAuth credentials enable access without giving the assistant the owner's Google password.

How data is used and shared

Google data is used to fulfill the owner's email, scheduling and personal administration requests. Relevant content may be sent to OpenAI for model inference. Responses, summaries and notifications may be delivered through Telegram. The assistant runtime and working data are hosted on DigitalOcean. These service providers process information under their respective policies.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sending email or creating events with attendees can disclose their contents to the recipients or attendees selected for that action.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the private agent server with restricted file access. Retrieved content and generated summaries may be retained in the assistant's conversation history, memory, working files or logs until the owner removes them. There is currently no fixed automatic deletion period for those records. Google, OpenAI, Telegram and the hosting provider maintain their own retention practices.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future access through that authorization but does not automatically delete information already stored by the assistant or messages already sent. The owner can remove the assistant's saved credentials, conversation history, memories and working files on the private server, and manage retained messages through the relevant service.

This website and contact

This informational website has no sign-in form, advertising, analytics scripts or connection to the assistant's Google credentials. Its hosting provider may process standard technical request logs. For questions or deletion requests, contact the operator using the support email shown on the application's Google authorization screen.

This policy will be updated if the installation's data practices change.
